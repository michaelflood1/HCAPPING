## communicatoin noise

any noise as distraction that affects how  a message is recieved

    External

        factors that are external to the receiver that interfere with ability to hear or listen

            - smell

            - background noise

            - room acoustics

            - being called upon by another client

    Physiological 

        things happening inside the body

            - pain

            - hunger

            - deaf

            - have to pee

            - decreased vision

    Psychological

        cognitive factors that interfere with receivers ability to accurately receive the message

            - emotions

            - culture

            - perceptions

            - insecurities
