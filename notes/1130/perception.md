Perceptions -- how you interpret -- the process of making sense of the world

    to become aware and have an understanding of a situation through our senses

eyes ear mouth nose skin

all pick up information that our body interprets

our brain then processes then we give it individualized meaning

4 stages of perception

selection

    we choose what we pay attention to ( gravitate to most intense)

organization

    clump and arrange what we selected from the environment into patterns

interpretation

    we assign meaning based on previous life experiences

negotiation

    this is the only step that involves others

    we communicate with others and sometimes adjust our understanding

common tendencies in perception

    fundamental attribution error

        tendency to give more weight to personal qualities than to the situation when making remarks ( we assume it is who they are rather than what they are going through)

    self serving bias

        quick to take credit for successes and assign blame elsewhere for failures

we are influenced by our perceptions

blind review

    we are influenced by oure expectations so offer a neutral presentation -- let people form their own opinion.

        shes awful and i get dissapointed

We are influenced by the obvious


Halo effect

    we cling to first impressions -- positive first impressions often influence our impression of someone / something

we assume others are like us
