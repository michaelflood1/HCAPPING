### trad vs critical thinking

trad

    preserves status quo

    accepts the norm and daily routine

    treats client and event in isolation

    does not make connections between clients and events

    limited scope and vision

    accepts the obvious without question


Critical

    asks why

    open to possibilities

    fair minded and honest in evaluation

    views each client and event as part of a larger group

    looks for patterns and trends

    wide scope and vision

    uses intuition and follows hunches

    seeks out experienced peers to use as sounding board when problem solving

### guidelines for crit thinking

ask

define

examine

analyze

avoid emotional reasoning

dont use either or

dont over simplify or generalize

consider other interpretations

tolerate uncertainty
