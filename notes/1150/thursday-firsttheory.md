### Chain of infection

Pathogen ==> Reservoir ==> Portal of exit ==> mode of transmission ==> port of entry ==> susceptible host

1. Pathogen
    - bacteria virus fungi parasite

2. Reservoir
    - where the pathogen lives ( on or in us, pets, food, water, clothes, objects)
    - warmth moisture darkness nutrients == uh oh

3. Portal of exit
    - How it leaves initial host
    - coughing, skin (touch), sneezing, bodily fluids 

4. mode of transmission
    - Contact : gloves gown
        - direct -- skin to skin
        - indirect touching same object
    - Droplet : mask eye pro, gloves, gown
        - when microbes are spread across short distance ( sub 1m)
        - sneeze, cough
    - Airborne : N95, gloves, gown eye pro ***before entry***
        - microbes transmitted long distance ( entire room )
        - common illness *TB*
        
    

5. port of entry
    - How it enters new host
    - mouth, nose, wounds, eyes etc

6 susceptible host
    - new person actually gets infected 
    - e.g weak or compromised immune system 
    
***goal***
BREAK THE CHAIN

Best way to break the chain is to practice proper hand hygeine
