### Nutrition

regular fluids are THIN

thickened fluids are THICK

#### thickened

3 types

honey thickened

nectar thickened

pudding



### SLP

texture of food/fluids decided by speech language pathologist (SLP) after conducting a **swallowing assessment**

### eating position

45-90degrees i.e high fowlers

must stay in this positioin for 30 minutes after eating or as per care plan


### care plan

some care plan will ask for fluids in and out to be monitored

this is all fluids taken by mouth iv tube feeding

e.g milk water soup ice cream popsicle

must melt to liquid to measure as fluid

output is the amount lost 

e.g urine, vomitus, diarrhea, drainage from wound

if intake greater than output may see swelling in feet may observe weight gain

if output greater than intake mayb appear shrivelled


common fluid restriction 1.5l over 24 hours

### NPO

Nothing by mouth

used if dysphagia,, upcoming surgery,, bloodwork

### enteral nutrition

people who cannot eat or swallow enough may be fed via tube

either goes into stomach ( gastric) 

or

Small intestine (J tubes)

why they might need a tube

failure to thrive

unconscious 

coma

stroke -- paralysis

severe dysphagia
