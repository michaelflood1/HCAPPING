# integumentary system

Skin is the biggest organ

protects, regulates, senses



### skin components

epidermus

    mainly skin cells replaced every 4 weeks from inner to outer filled with keratin

    outer layer : living and dead cells ; pigment

Dermis

    inner layer connective tissue

    has blood vessels nerves sweat oil glands hair roots


### functions of the skin

    injury germs chemicals uv water loss

absorption

    sunlight for vitamin d

    medications

excretion

### changes with age

loses elasticity

brown spots appear on hands // wrist

thicker nails

hair whitens and thins becomes dry

nerve endings decrease

fatty tissue lost

saggy skin fragility increases

wrinkles appear

oil and sweat glands secrete less

dry itchy skin

sensitive to temp changes

sensitivity to pain decreases

facial hair appears in some women

### promoting integumentary system health

no soap on face

moisturizing lotions ( best after a shower)

fluid intake

promote skin circulation

avoid longterm pressure

lotion twice a day

### wounds

a break in the skin or mucous membrane that becomes a portal for microbes

**abrasians**

partial thickness wound caused by rubbing or scraping

**contusion**

bruises ( closed wound caused by blow to body)

**incision**

clean cut 

**pressure ulcers**

    stage 1 the skin is red colour does not go normal after removal of pressure

    stage 2 affected area has a open wound

    stage 3 the dermis is gone

    stage 4 it has made it to the bone

**laeration**

jagged cut

**penetrating**
