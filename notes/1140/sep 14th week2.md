### AGING: What is normal

#### physical changes

- metabolism slows so less heat produced

- gradually lose ability to self repair

- lose elasticity strength and tone

- response time slows

- less body reserve so minor issues become major easily

**changes are individual and influence by many factors**


#### theories of aging

No single factor discovered to explain; complex process

Aging varies among individuals and within different systems of the same person

#### chronic illnesses

**known as persistant illnesses**

- ongoing illness, slow or gradual in onset, lasts long time

- cannot be cured, the focus is on preventing complications

i.e 

MS

Alzheimers

diabetes


#### acute illness

**immune system go brr**

fast to resolve

i.e

flu

cold


#### long term care

chronic illness can be managed and complications prevented

long term care is provided for the remainder of the clients life

goal of ltc is to help the resident cope with the challenges of living with a long term illness or disability while maintaining a good qol

#### rehabilitation

- rehab may be necessary after acute injury or illness or as part of treatment for chronic illness or disability

- can also maintain or slow decline of function

specific goals;

1. restore function to former level

2. improve functional ability

3. learn new skills

4. prevent further disability


#### rehab team

- client is the key member

- family, doctor, nursing team

- all work together to provide and plan a level of care

- families provide support and encouragement

- all parts of HCA job revolve around promoting client independance


#### role of family

counselling is provided to help cope with the many changes

 ### Pain

 #### what is pain

 an unpleasant sensory or emotional experience

 only the person in pain can state what the pain is for them

 sharp stabbing throbbing dull ache

#### why we feel


 bodys early warning system it may cause us to stop activities

 encourages res

 indicates return of sensation or nerve function

#### types

radiating

    pain felt beyond site of damage

phantom limb

    disruption of nerve endings in stumps felt where there is nothing

#### effect of age on pain

neonate may be less able to localize pain

ingant less able to express

individuals with expressive disorders less able to express

dementia may be less able to understand or localize

may be some decreased sensitivity with advanced age,, i.e receptors in skin
