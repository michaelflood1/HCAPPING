#### Bone

    covered by the periosteum ( a membrane)

    has a blood supply that delivers oxygen nutriens and removes waste

    bone marrow is found within spaces inside bones

    red bone marrow produces red blood cells

    approx 2million rbcs are produced every 2 seconds

### osteoporosis

bones become less dense and weaker

bone structure becomes porous fragile

increased risk of damage

common fracture sites hip spine wrist

risk factos

    increased age

    post menopausal estrogen decline

    genetics

    vitamin deficit

    lack of weight bearing activities

    smoking

    immobility

### arthritis

more than 100 different conditions 

common factor in joint and musculoskeletal pain

often loss of joint function

some forms also affect connective tissue

### osteoarthritis

causes cartilage to wear away decreases cushioning forcing bone to rub together

risk factor

    obesity and sedentary lifestyle

may see bone spurs

no cure

most common type

### rheumatoid arthritis

autoimmune disease

immune system attacks joint lining

causes inflammation pain swelling and stiffness

affects multiple joints i.e symmetrical

ongoing causes damage and elads to joint deformity

can affect other parts of the body

more common in women

can occur at any age

client comforting -- comfort through positioning, applying heat, meds safety self esteem independence weight loss walking aids
