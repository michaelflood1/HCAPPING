## weight

easy to measure but not whole story

muscle weighs more than fat

ideal weight is based on frame size

need to be consistent with clothing worn

varies with time of day and other factors

BMI

compares a persons weight in relation to their height

can be used as a screening tool

does not directly measure body fat or health

should be considered along with other health info


Waist to Hip ratio WHR

waist circumference / Hip circumference

provides info about fat distribution

more fat carried around the abdomen may be associated with health risks


### health risks

too little or too much body fat affects health

nutritional deficits, thermal regulation

high blood pressure // low blood pressure

diabetes

Cardiovascular disease

cancers

liver disease

joint and mobility problems
