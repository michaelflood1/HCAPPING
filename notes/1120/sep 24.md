## nutrition

refers to the many processes involved

ingestion -- intake

digestion -- what happens after food enters

absorption -- when digested it gets absorbed in blood

#### substances

fats proteins carbs vitamins minerals water

calories come from fats carbs and proteins

energy production is measured in calories

1g of carbohydrate = 4 cals

1g of protein = 4cals

1g of fat = 9 cals

### protein

Growth and repair of tissues

healing wounds

maintaining muscle

supporting the immune system

blood clotting

transporting substances in the blood including oxygen

Sources : meat poultry fish eggs milk yogurt chees

Plant sources : Beans lentils peas nuts seeds tofu and other soy products

### carbs

provides main source of energy, provides fiber which supports digestion

3 types

sugars -- simple fruit milk sweets

starches -- complex bread rice pasta potates

fibre whole grains beans veggies fruits nuts seeds

### fats

provide endergy

help vitamin absorption A D E K

protect organs and insulate the body

add flavour and help us feel full

3 types unsaturated saturated and trans fat

common sources

meat cheese butter oils

### vitamins and minerals

vitamins aid in normal function and growth

no calories

fat soluble( slower metabolism since through livers) can have toxic levels of fat soluble vitamins and water soluble (faster metabolism since through kidney)

lack of vitamin may cause illness

Minerals

Chemical substances found in plants and animals

each mineral has specific body functions 

iron helps with oxygen and muscles
